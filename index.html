Task 1
🌀 Make a Pattern Brush

Make a pattern brush from your drawing or collage.
Watch the video first. Then click a step to open it.

▶ Watch: How to make and apply a pattern brush
  1. Open your image in Photoshop
  2. Cut out the part you want to use
  3. Copy and paste it onto a new layer
  4. Delete the background
  1. Go to Image › Adjustments › Levels
  2. Move the sliders to make the image clearer
  1. Use the Elliptical Marquee Tool to select white areas
  2. Go to Select › Similar, then press Delete
  3. Deselect: Cmd/Ctrl + D
  4. Use the Crop Tool to crop tightly around your image
  5. Click the ✓ tick to apply
  1. Check the correct layer is selected
  2. Go to Edit › Define Pattern
  3. Give your pattern a clear name
  4. Click OK – your pattern is saved ✅
  1. Open a design page (or a blank page to test)
  2. Use the Pen Tool P to draw a path
  3. Create a new layer
  4. Go to Edit › Fill
  5. Choose Pattern and select your pattern
  6. Tick Script and choose Place Along Path
  7. Click OK
  • Change the size and spacing
  • Try different paths (straight, curved)
  • To remove the blue path line: open the Paths panel and delete the Work Path
  • Go to File › Export › Export As… › Format: JPG
  • Click Export
  • You need it for Task 3 and Padlet
💡 Take your time

Play and experiment. Good results come from trying different options.

Task 2
📄 Add to Your Design Pages

Add your pattern brush to a design page in Photoshop.

Page requirements

📋 Design development pages

  • Research links that connect to your design ideas
  • Clear subtitles (use a light monospaced font)

📋 Research pages

  • Different types of research
  • Clear subtitles
  • Written notes showing your understanding

How to use pattern brushes

Visual links

  • Connect related images
  • Guide the viewer's eye
  • Frame content areas

Balance

  • Don't overcrowd the page
  • Leave empty space
  • Balance text and images

The pattern is too strong.
Lower the layer Opacity. Or try a blend mode such as Multiply.

Drag the pattern layer below your images in the Layers panel.
